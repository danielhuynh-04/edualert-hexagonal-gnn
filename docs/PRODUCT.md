# PRODUCT CONTEXT — EduAlert

*Crafted in accordance with the [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) product definition standard.*

## 1. Product Identity & Purpose
- **Product Name:** EduAlert (Hệ thống Cảnh báo Sớm Kết quả Học tập Đa cấp độ)
- **Tagline:** A Multi-Level Early Academic Alert Platform Combining Deterministic Regulation Checking (Circular 08/2021) and Cellular Sheaf Attention Graph Neural Networks.
- **Core Purpose:** Shift academic advising from *reactive post-mortem failure discovery* (after final grades are recorded) to *proactive trajectory intervention* (weeks before students breach dismissal floors).
- **Domain:** Higher Education Learning Analytics (EdTech), Topological Deep Learning (Sheaf Theory on Graphs), and Regulatory Compliance.

## 2. Target Audiences & Primary Workflows
1. **Academic Advisors (Cố vấn Học tập - CVHT):**
   - *Needs:* Needs an immediate, prioritized triage list (Red / Yellow / Green) for their assigned classes. Needs to know *why* a student is slipping (factor attribution) and what specific counseling action to take (reduce credit load, enroll in peer-tutoring, retake prerequisite).
   - *Interaction Style:* High-density, zero-clutter, rapid scanning, one-click access to the individual diagnostic slip and meeting notes logger.
2. **Faculty Leadership (Ban Chủ nhiệm Khoa):**
   - *Needs:* Cohort-wide health metrics, course bottleneck identification (which courses have historical fail rates >20%), and demographic fairness reports across admission methods (THPT, Học bạ, ĐGNL).
3. **Office of Academic Affairs (Phòng Đào tạo):**
   - *Needs:* 100% legally grounded compliance verification under Circular 08/2021/TT-BGDĐT, batch CSV import with line-by-line sanity audits, and formal CSV/Excel export for the disciplinary board.

## 3. Non-Negotiable System Invariants
- **Compliance Priority Invariant (Quy tắc Vàng):** If any condition of Circular 08/2021 (R01–R05) is triggered, the student is unconditionally `HIGH_RISK` (Red). Machine learning models can *elevate* risk early, but can *never* downgrade or dismiss a regulatory violation.
- **Explainability Over Black-Box Prediction:** No ungrounded statistical scores. Every risk label must be accompanied by decomposed SHAP factor contributions, top Sheaf Attention relational edges, and deterministic template natural language explanations. Zero LLM numerical hallucination.
- **Student Privacy & Ethics (Nghị định 13/2023/NĐ-CP):** Student identities are hashed with SHA-256 for offline training; access is partitioned strictly via Row-Level Security (RLS) so advisors only see their assigned students; all write actions are immutably logged to `audit_log`.

## 4. Voice, Tone & Persona
- **Authoritative & Institutional:** Echoes the gravity of a university registrar's official seal and academic board proceedings.
- **Supportive & Constructive:** Never uses stigmatizing or accusatory language. Alerts are framed as diagnostic opportunities for timely academic assistance.
- **Scientifically Rigorous:** Displays uncertainty intervals ($\pm 0.15$ GPA), calibration curves (ECE), and permutation test baselines ($R^2 \to 0.024$ under label shuffle) to demonstrate technical honesty.
